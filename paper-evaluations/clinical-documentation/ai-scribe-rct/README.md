## Paper

Randomized Clinical Trial of Ambient AI Scribes for Clinical Documentation

## Study Type

Randomized clinical trial

## Clinical Question

Can ambient AI scribes reduce physician documentation burden and improve the clinical workflow without introducing unacceptable documentation errors or additional workload?

## 1. Clinical Problem and Rationale

Clinical documentation is a major component of physician workload and contributes to cognitive burden and physician burnout. Ambient AI scribes could potentially reduce this burden by generating clinical documentation that can be reviewed and edited by physicians.

Reducing documentation time may allow physicians to spend more time engaging with patients and potentially improve workflow efficiency. However, the clinical value of an AI scribe should not be evaluated only by the reduction in documentation time.

Important considerations include **accuracy, omissions, verification burden, physician workload, patient interaction, safety, cost, and long-term sustainability**.

The key clinical evaluation question is therefore whether AI-generated documentation produces a meaningful **net benefit** rather than simply reducing the time required to generate a note.
